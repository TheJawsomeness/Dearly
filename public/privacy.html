Dearly Privacy Policy

Effective date: Oct 15, 2026

Dearly respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use Dearly and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

When you use Dearly, we may process the following information:

  • Account information made available through Google sign-in.
  • Profile information, such as your display name.
  • Posts and content you submit.
  • Voting activity, including likes and dislikes.
  • Technical information needed to operate and protect the app.

How We Use Information

  • To sign you in and maintain your account.
  • To display profiles, posts, and votes.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve Dearly.
  • To troubleshoot problems and prevent abuse.

Service Providers

Dearly uses Supabase for authentication and database services and may use GitHub Pages or Vercel for hosting.

Sharing and Visibility

Posts and other information displayed publicly may be visible to other users. Do not publish information you do not want others to see.

Data Retention and Deletion

To request access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact the app operator using the contact details below.

Security

We use technical safeguards intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Dearly changes. Updates will be published on this page.
